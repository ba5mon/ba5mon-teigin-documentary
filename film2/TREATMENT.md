# The Teigin Incident — Treatment (Paper-cut Lightbox)

Style: **Paper-cut Lightbox** (`styles/paper-lantern/STYLE.md`), all invariants kept: every shot is one shallow box of parallel cut-paper sheets lit from behind; back layers pale and translucent, front layers near-black; soft cast shadows between layers; light is the only saturated element; motion is paper on a stick (slide, rotate, grow from an edge, light up); dissolves, iris, light off/on, near-plane curtains; never whip pans.

The production script (user, 11 scenes, locked) sets the direction, and this treatment follows it exactly:
- **Silhouettes only**: people are jointed paper figures cut from the dark front sheets; never faces.
- **Muted sepia and grey**: each scene's stack has one temperature (a sepia ladder for interiors and dusk, a grey ladder for winter streets and the prison). The one accent is a dull **seal red**, used only where the script asks for red (the third pin, the THEORY stamp) and for the twelve.
- **Light film grain** on top of the lens (a fine grain at mux), and **slow camera pans**: lateral travel with depth parallax, slow dolly in/out.
- **Title cards** (`Chapter N: …`, 3 s, no narration): the lamp switches off; in the dark box a single cut strip of white paper burns with the chapter title. Full-screen black, white letters, as the script asks. Scenes marked "TITLE CARD: NONE" follow on with a dissolve.
- **Main title** after the cold open (5 s): "THE TEIGIN INCIDENT" / "The Imperial Bank Poisoning, 1948", the same dark-box card.
- **Final card** after the last line: "The case remains officially closed. The questions remain open." white on black.
- **On-screen text** as the script lists it: dates and labels are cut paper strips that grow from their edge inside the box; the opening place/date line is typed in a typewriter face, bottom left, as the script asks.
- No graphic images of death: the cups, the empty room, a fade to dark and the counter.

Narration: the script's text, word for word; recorded with Kokoro `am_michael` (every sentence already exists from the previous version and passed the speech check). Subtitles: one quiet burned-in line under the box + `.srt`. Runtime ≈ 8:20.

## 1. Three candidate structures

The script fixes the order, so the choice is how the box carries it.
1. **One box, many sheets**: every scene is a new stack slid into the same lamp; the light going off is the chapter break. Clear, consistent with the black title cards.
2. **The box as keepsake**: open on a real lamp on a table in a dark room, push into the box, return to it at the end. Elegant, but the script opens "directly" on the street scene and ends on a black card.
3. **A vertical stack**: the film tilts down through sky, city, bank, cell. Fights the script's scene list.

**Chosen: 1.** One lamp; each scene is a stack; chapter cards are the lamp switching off with one lit strip of title; scenes without a card dissolve. The opening and ending come from the script: in on the bank front at 3:00, out on the same bank front empty at dusk, then the lamp goes out and the last card burns alone.

## 2. Logline and arc
A man with an armband talks sixteen people into drinking poison and walks out with the cash; the painter convicted of it waits 32 years on death row for a signature that never comes, and the questions stay open.
Setup (1–4) → turn (5–8: the trail, the painter, the confession) → doubt and ending (9–11).

## 3. Benchmark

## 6. Cue map
- Instruments (paper-lantern palette, topic's culture): felt piano, a plucked zither (koto-like, physical model), harp, celesta / music box, soft strings; single soft strokes only (woodblock, small gong).
- 1–3: zither phrases in D minor pentatonic over a soft string pedal; the walk in 3 is paced by one woodblock stroke per step, the knock is three woodblock strokes; then silence.
- 4: strings only, thinning; near-silence from “They did as he said”; the fade to dark is held silent; one small gong stroke under “12 dead · 4 survived”.
- 5–7: felt piano returns, slow; the walk-out in 5 is unaccompanied but for footsteps on snow; pins = celesta notes; detectives' knock = three soft knocks.
- 8: a low string drone; the lamp swing as a slow pendulum (tick + chain creak).
- 9: dark harp and low strings; the THEORY stamp = a dry card-stock tap.
- 10–11: piano and zither return; the corridor walk with soft footsteps; the final card holds one chord, then room tone, then silence.
- Music ducks ~5–6 dB under speech (style mix), foley low.
