## Locked facts (must stay accurate everywhere, including on-screen text)

- 16 people drank the liquid; 10 died at the bank, 2 in hospital (12 dead), 4 survived.
- The killer wore a health-official armband and presented a business card, claiming GHQ had ordered dysentery prevention.
- He took about ¥164,000 in cash and a cheque for about ¥17,000, which was cashed the next day.
- Earlier attempts: Yasuda Bank (October 1947) and Mitsubishi Bank (19 January 1948). The Dr. Shigeru Matsui card trail led to Sadamichi Hirasawa.
- Hirasawa was arrested in August 1948 in Otaru, confessed after weeks of interrogation, then recanted. Sentenced to death in 1950, upheld in 1955, never executed. Died 10 May 1987, aged 95, in a prison hospital.
- The Unit 731 / Noborito link is a theory, not proven; always labelled THEORY · NOT PROVEN on screen.
