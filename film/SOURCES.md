## Map — US Army Map Service (US federal work, public domain)
- `raw/maps/ams_tokyo_08.jpg`: *Japan City Plans 1:12,500, Tokyo and Environs, Sheet 8 “Koishikawa”* (AMS L902, compiled 1945–46). Perry-Castañeda Library Map Collection, University of Texas at Austin: https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/ams/japan_city_plans/txu-oclc-6549645-08.jpg (index: https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/ams/japan_city_plans/index_tokyo.html). Its legend separates “Built-up area” from “Bombed area”. Shiinamachi Station appears on the Musashino Railroad near the sheet's west edge.

## Text
- Constitution of Japan (1946, in force 3 May 1947), Article 38, official English translation. Laws are not copyrightable (Japanese Copyright Act, art. 13). Typeset in the film and labelled as a transcription.
